From the practice

Two days of practice life on camera

Last week we welcomed a film crew from the Bertelsmann Stiftung's HealthTransformationHub to our practice.

Last week we welcomed a film crew from the Bertelsmann Stiftung's HealthTransformationHub to our practice. They followed our everyday work for two days, focusing on three things that are central to what we do:

How we are organised as a team practice

Different professional groups take on responsibility according to their qualifications and work closely together.

How we work together

Short distances, mutual coordination and a shared understanding of care bring together the strengths of the team.

Our concept of modern patient care

We want to offer care that goes beyond traditional roles and uses the strengths of the whole team, always aiming to support our patients as well as possible.

The filming was an exciting chance to see our daily work from the outside. A big thank you to the Media2Move camera team, the HealthTransformationHub team and our whole team for their commitment on those days.

We look forward to the finished film and will gladly show it to you as soon as it is released.

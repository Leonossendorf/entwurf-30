Merry Christmas
Practice closed

Christmas break

Our practices in Ochtrup and Gronau-Epe are closed between Christmas and New Year. Local GP practices will cover for us.

Dear patients,

between Christmas and New Year our team is taking a short break. Both practices, in Ochtrup and in Gronau-Epe, will be closed during this time.

Who can help me during this time?

  • Local GP practices will cover for us.
  • In the evenings, at night and at weekends you can reach the out-of-hours medical service on 116 117.
  • In life-threatening emergencies, please call 112 immediately.

Prescriptions and referrals

Please remember in good time before the break to order any repeat prescriptions you will need over the holidays. You can order online via medflex as usual. How ordering works.

We wish you a merry Christmas and a good, healthy start to the new year!

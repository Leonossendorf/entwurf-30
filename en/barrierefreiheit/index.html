Accessibility statement

Our website should be easy to use for everyone, including people with impaired sight, hearing or movement. We follow the WCAG 2.2 guidelines at level AA.

Draft: this statement is a sample and must be checked before publication.

What helps you on this site

  • Text in three sizes
  • High contrast
  • Reading the current page aloud
  • Full keyboard operation
  • Image descriptions for screen readers
  • A page in plain language

What is not yet accessible

  • Our film does not have subtitles yet.
  • Doctolib and medflex are services from other companies. We have no influence on their accessibility. You can always call us instead.
  • We will add details of step-free access to our practices.

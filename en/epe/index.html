Our practice in Epe

In June 2024 we opened a second practice in Gronau-Epe. It secures GP care in the region and also takes pressure off our main practice in Ochtrup.

What we offer in Epe

  • GP care
  • Acute and infection clinic, by appointment only
  • Appointment clinic by arrangement
  • Prescriptions and referrals via medflex

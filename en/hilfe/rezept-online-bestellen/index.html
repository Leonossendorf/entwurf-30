Order a prescription online

How to order repeat prescriptions and referrals via medflex

Order a repeat prescription without coming in

Do you take a medicine regularly and need a new prescription? Or do you need a referral to a specialist? You can order both via medflex from home. Requests are possible from Monday 7 am to Friday 12 noon. It costs you nothing.

Open medflex

What you need

  • A phone, tablet or computer with internet access
  • Your mobile number
  • The packet or the name of your medicine (ideally with the strength, e.g. “100 mg”)

Open the medflex page

On our website, tap “Order prescription”. The medflex page opens with our practice name. Scroll down a little to the box “Wie können wir Ihnen helfen?” (How can we help you?).

Choose “Folgerezept anfragen”

Tap “Folgerezept anfragen” (request a repeat prescription). For a referral, choose “Überweisung anfragen”. Then tap “Weiter” (next).

Choose your practice

Choose the practice where you are a patient: Ochtrup or Epe. Then tap “Weiter” (next).

Confirm that you are already a patient

Tick “Ja, ich bin bereits Patient” (yes, I am already a patient) and tap “Bestätigen” (confirm).

New to us? Then please make an appointment first.

Enter your medicine and contact details

medflex now asks which medicine you need and for your contact details. Fill in everything and follow the instructions on the screen. Finally, send the request.

Wait for the reply

We reply in writing. Without registering, you get our message by text and can reply to it directly. You can redeem an electronic prescription with your health insurance card at any pharmacy.

Not working?

No problem. Call us on 02553 93 97 0. We will be glad to help.

Does ordering via medflex cost anything?

No. It is free for you.

Do I have to register with medflex?

No. You can use medflex without registering. You then get our reply by text. If you like, you can install the free medflex app and see all your messages at a glance.

I am new to the practice. Can I still order?

You can only order prescriptions and referrals if you are already being treated by us. Please make an appointment first.

Why can I not order at the weekend?

From Friday 12 noon to Monday 7 am, medflex does not accept requests. We deal with everything during practice hours, so it is best to order your prescription in good time.

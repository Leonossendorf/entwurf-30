Order a prescription online

How to order repeat prescriptions and referrals via medflex

Order a repeat prescription without coming to the practice

Do you take a medication regularly and need a new prescription? Or do you need a referral to a specialist? You can order this free of charge via medflex from home. This is possible from Monday at 7 am to Friday at 12 noon.

Which guide is right for you?

Answer a few short questions. You will then only see the steps that apply to you.

Have you been treated at our practice before?
How would you like to use medflex?

Your guide:

Go to the guide Practise first

What you need

  • a phone, tablet or computer with internet access
  • your mobile number
  • the packet or the name of your medication (ideally with the strength, e.g. “100 mg”)

Open the medflex page

Please read the whole guide to the end first. The button opens medflex in a new window or in the app. You will then usually no longer see this guide.

Once you have read everything, tap here:

Open medflex

Our practice name is at the top. You do not need an account and do not have to log in.

Choose “Folgerezept anfragen”

Scroll down to the box “Wie können wir Ihnen helfen?” (How can we help you?). Tap “Folgerezept anfragen” (repeat prescription), or “Überweisung anfragen” for a referral. Then tap “Weiter” (next).

Can’t find the box? From Friday at 12 noon to Monday at 7 am it is hidden and you only see a yellow notice. That is not an error, please try again from Monday at 7 am.

Choose your practice

Under “Wer soll Ihre Anfrage erhalten?” (who should receive your request) choose the practice where you are a patient: Ochtrup or Epe. Then tap “Weiter” (next).

Confirm that you are already a patient

Tap the box next to “Ja, ich bin bereits Patient” (yes, I am already a patient). The tick appears and the grey button “Bestätigen” (confirm) turns blue. Tap it.

Enter your medication and contact details

Enter the medication you need and then your contact details. Follow the instructions on the screen. Finally, send the request.

Wait for the reply

We deal with your request during our opening hours. Without an account, you get our reply by text and can respond to it directly. You can redeem an electronic prescription with your health insurance card at any pharmacy.

Log in to medflex

Please read the whole guide to the end first. The button opens medflex in a new window or in the app. You will then usually no longer see this guide.

Once you have read everything, tap here:

Go to medflex login

Already on our medflex page? Then tap “Einloggen” (log in) at the top right.

Enter your email address and password

Enter your email address and password. Then tap “Als Patient einloggen” (log in as a patient).

Forgotten your password? Tap “Neues Passwort festlegen” (set a new password).

Choose our practice and make a request

Choose our practice in your contact list. At the bottom of the chat, tap “Anfrage stellen” (make a request) and then “Folgerezept anfragen” (repeat prescription).

Enter your medication and send

Enter the medication and the pack size. Choose whether you want to collect the prescription at the practice or receive it via medflex. Then send the request.

Read the reply

You will find our reply in medflex, in your chat with our practice.

Open the medflex page

Please read the whole guide to the end first. The button opens medflex in a new window or in the app. You will then usually no longer see this guide.

Once you have read everything, tap here:

Open medflex

Have you already received an invitation from us by text or email? Then tap the link in it instead and continue with step 3.

Choose “Über medflex verbinden”

Scroll down to the box “Wie können wir Ihnen helfen?” (How can we help you?). Tap “Über medflex verbinden” (connect via medflex) and then “Weiter” (next).

Can’t find the box? It is hidden from Friday at 12 noon to Monday at 7 am. Please try again from Monday at 7 am.

Enter your details

Follow the instructions on the screen. Enter your details and your email address, and choose a password. Keep a note of it somewhere safe.

Confirm your email address

You receive an email from medflex. Tap the link in it. No email? Check your spam folder. Your account is then connected to our practice.

Log in and get started

From now on, log in here:

Go to medflex login

The guide for account holders shows you how to order a prescription with an account.

First, come and see us once

You can only order prescriptions and referrals online once you have been treated by us. So please make your first appointment first. Our guide takes you through everything else:

Make your first appointment New here

After your first visit

Once you have been to see us, you can do everything online. Just come back to this page and answer the question above with “Yes”: guide for patients.

Still unsure?

Try it out first, nothing is sent: Practise now. You will find answers to common questions just below.

Still have a question? You can reach us on 02553 93 97 0.

Does ordering via medflex cost anything?

No. It is free of charge for you.

Do I have to register with medflex?

No. You can order prescriptions without an account. You then get our reply by text. With an account, you see all your messages in one place. How to create an account.

I am new to the practice. Can I still order?

Not yet. Prescriptions and referrals are only available online after your first visit. How to make your first appointment.

Why can’t I see the box on medflex?

From Friday at 12 noon to Monday at 7 am, medflex does not accept requests. During this time the box “Wie können wir Ihnen helfen?” is hidden and you only see the yellow notice. So it is best to order your prescription in good time.

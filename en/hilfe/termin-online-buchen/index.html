How to book an appointment via Doctolib

Book an appointment whenever it suits you

On Doctolib you can see our free appointments and book the one that suits you. This works around the clock and is free of charge. Our practices work by appointment only. You also need an appointment for the acute clinic.

Which guide is right for you?

Answer a few short questions. You will then only see the steps that apply to you.

Have you been treated at our practice before?
How are you insured?
Do you have a Doctolib account?

Your guide:

Go to the guide Practise first

What you need

  • a phone, tablet or computer with internet access
  • an email address or your mobile number
  • for infection appointments: an FFP2 mask when you visit

Open the Doctolib page

Please read the whole guide to the end first. The button opens Doctolib in a new window or in the app. You will then usually no longer see this guide.

Once you have read everything, tap here:

Open Doctolib

If a notice about cookies appears, just tap “ABLEHNEN” (decline). You can still book.

Tap “TERMIN BUCHEN”

You will see our practice name. Tap the blue button “TERMIN BUCHEN” (book an appointment).

Choose the specialty

For an appointment with our doctors, choose “Hausarzt / Allgemeinmediziner” (GP). For a flu or Covid vaccination, choose “Medizinischer Fachangestellter (MFA)”.

Answer “Ja” to the question about your visit

Doctolib asks whether you have been to us before. Tap “Ja” (yes). The question is about the person coming to the appointment, for example your child or your mother.

Answer “Nein” to the question about your visit

Doctolib asks whether you have been to us before. Tap “Nein” (no). The question is about the person coming to the appointment.

Choose the location

Choose Ochtrup or Gronau-Epe. If either is fine, tap “Ich habe keine Präferenz” (no preference).

Choose your insurance

Tap “Gesetzlich versichert” (statutory) or “Privat versichert” (private).

Choose a category

For a planned appointment, choose “Terminsprechstunde”. If you are ill, choose “Akut- und Infektsprechstunde”. In Epe it is called “Sprechstunde Epe”.

Choose the appointment type and doctor

Tap the type of appointment, for example “Sprechstunde” (consultation). Then choose your doctor. If you do not mind, tap “Ich habe keine Präferenz” (no preference).

Choose a time

You will see the free appointments. Tap the time that suits you.

Tap “EINLOGGEN”

Doctolib now asks for your account. Tap the yellow button “EINLOGGEN” (log in).

Enter your email or mobile number and password

Enter the email address or mobile number of your account and tap “Weiter” (next). Then enter your password.

Tap “REGISTRIEREN”

You need a free Doctolib account to book. Under “Neu auf Doctolib?” (new to Doctolib) tap “REGISTRIEREN” (register).

Create your account

Enter your email address or mobile number and tap “Weiter” (next). Doctolib then guides you through the final steps: you receive a code, choose a password and enter your name and date of birth.

If you already have an account after all, Doctolib will tell you and you can log in.

Confirm the appointment

Check the details and confirm the appointment. Doctolib also asks who the appointment is for. You then receive a confirmation.

Please make your first appointment by phone

If you are a new patient with statutory insurance, online booking is not yet possible. Please call us:

Call 02553 93 97 0

At the top of the page you can see whether we are open right now. On the phone, just say: “I am new and would like a first appointment.”

What Doctolib shows in this case

If you try on Doctolib anyway, you will see this message. It is not an error and you have not done anything wrong.

After that, everything works online

After your first visit you can book appointments via Doctolib and order prescriptions via medflex. Then answer the first question above with “Yes”.

Still unsure?

Try it out first, nothing is sent: Practise now. You will find answers to common questions just below.

Still have a question? You can reach us on 02553 93 97 0.

I am new. Why can’t I book online?

On Doctolib, booking is not available for new patients with statutory insurance. Please call us for your first appointment: 02553 93 97 0. Privately insured patients can also book online as new patients.

I cannot find a free appointment for the acute clinic.

Appointments for the acute clinic are released 24 hours in advance. Check again the day before, or give us a call.

Can I book an appointment for a relative?

Yes. Doctolib asks who the appointment is for when you book, so you can book for your child or your parents, for example.

How do I cancel an appointment?

In your Doctolib account under “Meine Termine” (my appointments), or by phone. Please cancel in good time so that other patients can use the appointment.

The text with the code does not arrive.

Check that your mobile number is correct and wait a minute or two. Sometimes it helps to request the code again.

I have forgotten my password.

When logging in, tap “Passwort vergessen?” (forgotten password). Doctolib will send you an email so you can set a new password.

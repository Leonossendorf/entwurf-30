How to book an appointment via Doctolib

Book an appointment when it suits you

Doctolib shows you our free appointments and you book the right one straight away. It works around the clock and is free. Our practices work by appointment, and you also need an appointment for the acute clinic.

Open Doctolib

What you need

  • A phone, tablet or computer with internet access
  • An email address and your mobile number
  • For infection appointments: an FFP2 mask for your visit

Open the Doctolib page

On our website, tap “Book online”. The Doctolib page opens. If a cookie notice appears, simply tap “Ablehnen” (decline). You can still book.

Tap “Termin buchen”

You will see our practice name. Tap the blue button “Termin buchen” (book appointment).

Choose the specialty

For an appointment with our doctors, choose “Hausarzt / Allgemeinmediziner” (GP).

Answer the question about your first visit

Have you been to us before? Tap “Ja” (yes) or “Nein” (no). The question is about the person coming to the appointment, for example your child or your mother.

Choose the reason and time

Choose the reason for your visit, for example the acute clinic. You will then see the free appointments. Tap the time that suits you.

Log in and confirm

Finally, log in to Doctolib. The first time, you create a free account with your email address and mobile number. Then confirm the appointment and you will get a confirmation by email.

Not working?

No problem. Call us on 02553 93 97 0. We will find an appointment together.

I cannot find a free appointment for the acute clinic.

Appointments for the acute clinic are released 24 hours in advance. Check again the day before, or give us a call.

Can I book an appointment for a relative?

Yes. Doctolib asks who the appointment is for when you book, so you can book for your child or your parents, for example.

How do I cancel an appointment?

In your Doctolib account under “Meine Termine” (my appointments), or by phone. Please cancel in good time so that other patients can use the appointment.

The text with the code does not arrive.

Check that your mobile number is correct and wait a minute or two. Sometimes it helps to request the code again. If it still does not work, call us and we will be happy to book the appointment for you.

I have forgotten my password.

When logging in, tap “Passwort vergessen?” (forgotten password). Doctolib will send you an email so you can set a new password.

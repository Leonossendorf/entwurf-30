Shape the future with us

Discover career opportunities and grow in an inspiring environment

We are a growing team practice in the Münsterland with more than 60 staff at our locations in Ochtrup and Gronau-Epe.

We train apprentices, help career changers into their dream job and, as an academic teaching practice of the universities of Duisburg-Essen and Münster, support the next generation of doctors. Apply for an open position or convince us with a speculative application.

Apprenticeship as a medical assistant Starting 1 August 2027

Do you get on well with all kinds of people? Do you enjoy helping others? Does working in a modern GP practice team sound exciting? Then apply now.

What we offer

  • An interesting and varied apprenticeship
  • Close support throughout your training
  • Many opportunities to learn and develop
  • A friendly, professionally skilled team

Your profile

  • At least a secondary school certificate (Fachoberschulreife), Abitur or Fachhochschulreife welcome
  • Enjoy working with people and good communication skills
  • Willingness to work in a team and take responsibility
  • Basic IT skills
Apply for this position
Specialist in paediatrics (m/f/d) Full or part time, moving into general practice possible

Our medical team is made up of nine doctors from different generations. Alongside GP and diabetes care, paediatrics, sports medicine, geriatrics and naturopathy are important core areas.

What we offer

  • A paediatric focus, also beyond your training period
  • A young team with flat hierarchies
  • Flexible, predictable working hours
  • Help finding housing and childcare locally

You are a good fit if you

  • Are a specialist or in advanced training (at least 5th year) in paediatrics
  • Are interested in moving into general practice
  • Hold a German licence to practise medicine
